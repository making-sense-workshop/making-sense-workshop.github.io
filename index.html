01The workshop

Foundation models are
reaching the senses.

Bringing together

  • Health sensing
  • Ubiquitous computing
  • Audio
  • Robotics
  • Industrial sensing
  • Time series

Wearables stream heart rhythms, motion and sleep from hundreds of millions of wrists. Microphones and earables capture speech, breathing and coughs. Tactile and olfactory sensors let robots and assistants feel and smell. Factories, vehicles and grids emit continuous telemetry.

Sensor foundation models are now deployed to millions of users. However, they are built in silos, one per application community, and the questions that decide whether multisensory intelligence works remain open. How should heterogeneous sensors be aligned with language? How should missing data and device heterogeneity be handled at scale? Which sensors carry unique or synergistic information? Do sensor models reason or merely recognize? How can they be deployed safely on the body?

02Topics

The questions
that remain open.

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to, the following.

  1. Wearable and biosignal foundation models (PPG, ECG, EEG, EMG)
  2. Sensor-language models and reasoning agents
  3. Tokenization and pretraining for heterogeneous sensors
  4. Missing modalities, irregular sampling and device heterogeneity
  5. Audio, tactile, olfactory and embodied sensing
  6. Egocentric, ambient and industrial telemetry sensing
  7. Multisensory fusion and transfer across speech, vision and sensor models
  8. Benchmarks, datasets and evaluation of sensor reasoning
  9. On-device, efficient, cost-aware and dynamic sensing
  10. Personal-health agents, clinical validation, privacy and safety on the body

03Call for papers

Papers, demos
and datasets.

Submissions use the AAAI-27 template and are reviewed double-blind, so please anonymize your paper. We accept two kinds of paper.

Track 01

Research papers

Novel methods, theory or experiments. Eligible for the Best Paper Award.

Track 02

Demo and dataset papers

New datasets, benchmarks or demonstrable systems. Eligible for the Best Demo or Dataset Award.

Long papers
Up to 7 pages of technical content, with unlimited pages for references and appendices
Short papers
Up to 4 pages of technical content, with unlimited pages for references and appendices
Supplementary material
Allowed, but reviewers are not required to review it
Awards
Judged on the quality of the work, regardless of page length
